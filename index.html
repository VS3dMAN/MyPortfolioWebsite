03
Board Game Multiplayer Live

Splendor Duel

The gem-trading duel rebuilt for the browser — then stretched far past its two-player box limits. Plays 2 to 8, free-for-all at every size, teams at the even ones, with a rules engine parameterised by seat count so the economy holds at any table.

This one is engineered, not just built: a fuzzer hammers the engine for deadlocks, a chaos suite attacks the UI, and a heuristic bot played 800 games per table size to prove seat fairness before anything shipped. Turn clock with auto-move, lobby with live seat management, full light/dark theme — running serverless on Cloudflare Workers.
